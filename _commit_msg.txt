PSD dock: y-zoom, method label, clean spines, A-series tiles

Brings the per-epoch PSD grid up to V2's interaction and styling:

* Up/Down arrows zoom a y-axis shared across all epoch tiles. Added the
  YZoomMixin contract to EpochGridView (gated by Y_ZOOM): _build_grid
  adopts one y-max over the tiles and _set_y_top rescales them together.
* The PSD method name (e.g. "Welch", "Carspan strict") is drawn in the
  upper-left corner of each tile, and the top/right spines are removed
  (draw_psd_tile).
* Tiles take a landscape A-series aspect (height = width/√2) via a
  heightForWidth container (TILE_ASPECT), so they stay readable and the
  grid scrolls vertically when they overflow.

Only the PSD dock opts into Y_ZOOM / TILE_ASPECT; the profile, transfer
and spectrogram grids are unchanged.
