Poincare: delegate annotations to mplcursors (V2 behaviour)

The dock hand-rolled annotations with ax.annotate plus custom click
handling, so they could not be dragged and removal was unreliable. V2
delegated this to mplcursors (multiple=True), which makes each
annotation draggable and removes it on a right-click. Restored that:
a cursor is (re)built over the per-epoch scatter clouds each redraw and
an add-callback fills in the epoch / IBI pair / time. The double-click
jump to the pre-processing dock is kept as a separate button_press
handler.
