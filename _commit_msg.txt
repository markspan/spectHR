View menu: grey + close docks lacking their source channel

When a loaded recording has no blood-pressure channel the BP dock is now
closed and its View-menu entry greyed out (and likewise the Transfer /
Transfer-Profile docks when neither respiration nor BP is present). The
entry re-enables for a later recording that carries the channel.
_DOCK_REQUIRES maps a dock to a session predicate; docks not listed are
always available since they derive from the R-peaks. Added an offscreen
MainWindow test.
