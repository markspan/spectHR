HR series: shade inhalation phases behind the trace (V2)

The HR tachogram now shades each inhalation interval (session.breath
INH windows) in light blue behind the trace, as V2 did, so the
respiratory sinus arrhythmia (HR rising on inhalation, falling on
exhalation) is legible. The inhalation windows are carried on HRModel
and clipped to the visible window when drawn. Relies on the breath
phases now produced by apply_breath_phases during loading.
