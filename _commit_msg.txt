Poincare: right-click an annotation to remove it

Single-click annotates a point; there was no way to clear an annotation
short of redrawing. Right-clicking (button 3) over an annotation now
removes that artist. Factored the proximity test into
_annotation_index_near so both the right-click delete and the
double-click jump share one hit-test.
