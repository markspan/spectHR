Make the spectral tiles V2-faithful (PSD CI/band fills, Bode transfer)

Port the V2 plotting routines into a shared display helper
(spectUI/common/spectral_plots.py) and use them so the spectral docks match
V2 in functionality and look:

- PSD       : confidence-interval shading, black PSD line, per-band
  under-curve fills labelled with their integrated band power, a unit-bearing
  y-axis (0..peak) and the band-table x-range.  Computes with CI now.
- Profiles  : per-band lines in the configured band colours, unit y-axis,
  epoch-relative time, y starting at 0.
- Transfer  : a Bode triple per tile — |H| (with PSD-style band fills),
  phase, and coherence with the min-coherence threshold line — sharing the
  frequency axis.

Band power for the legends uses spectHR.band_power_rectangular; the helper is
display-only (matplotlib), the algorithms stay in spectHR.

Combined with the workspace-change propagation, the spectral docks now
recompute and redraw when the bands / PSD method / transfer settings change.

401 passing (grid test asserts the Bode triple and the settings re-resolve).
