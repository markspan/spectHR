Add Profiles, Spectrogram, Transfer and Transfer-profile grid docks

Four more per-epoch docks on the EpochGridView base, each calling its
verified spectHR analysis entry point with workspace settings (compute
nothing in the UI):

- ProfilePlotWidget          : compute_band_power_profile (band power vs time).
- SpectrogramPlotWidget      : fetch_spectrogram (PSD grid as a heatmap).
- TransferPlotWidget         : compute_transfer (|H| + coherence vs frequency).
- TransferProfilePlotWidget  : compute_transfer_profile (per-band |H| vs time).

The transfer docks select their input channel (respiration / BP) from
TransferAnalysis.input_signal and depend additionally on BP/RESP changes.
A shared _view() helper on EpochGridView resolves the WorkspaceView.

Wired into MainWindow data_specs.  Tested: each builds one tile per active
epoch off-thread.  400 passing.
