Transfer Bode tiles: band shading + phase axis in pi (V2)

Brought the transfer (Bode) tiles up to V2's look:

* Vertical band shading (axvspan) across the phase and coherence panels,
  coloured from the band table.
* Phase panel reads in pi: wrapped phase plotted as coherence-gated
  points (low-coherence bins faded by coherence_mask_alpha), y-axis
  ticked at -pi .. pi with pi labels and a zero line.
* Modulus keeps the PSD-style under-curve band fills and now labels its
  y-axis with the input-dependent unit (modulus_unit); coherence shows
  the min-coherence threshold line; top/right spines removed on all three.
* TransferProfile lines are coloured by their band colour.

All driven by the existing TransferAnalysis settings (phase_view,
coherence_mask_alpha, show_coherence_threshold, min_coherence, f_min/f_max).
