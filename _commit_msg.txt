Match V2 PSD display: connected band fills + CARSPAN smoothing

Two display-layer regressions made the dev PSD tiles differ from V2,
even though the carspan / carspan_strict compute path is byte-identical
to V2 (the engine, _carspan.py and _smoothing.py are unchanged):

* Band fills left gaps. V2 extends each band's fill polygon to the
  midpoint shared with its neighbour so the coloured bands connect at
  the boundaries, while still integrating band power over the
  configured edges. Ported V2's _band_draw_extents into draw_band_fills
  (interpolated endpoints, FullRange keeps its own range).

* CARSPAN spectra were drawn unsmoothed. V2 applies the plot-only
  3-point moving average (smooth3) to the displayed curve and CI when
  smooth_for_display is set; the dev tile skipped it, so carspan_strict
  plots looked jagged versus V2's. draw_psd_tile now smooths the display
  curve/CI (band power still integrated on the raw periodogram), the PSD
  dock passes the flag for carspan/carspan_strict, and the dev default
  smooth_for_display is restored to True to match V2.

Also added V2's dashed CI edge lines and CI-aware y-scaling (CI lifts
the limit up to 3x the peak) so the tiles read like V2's.
