Add the epoch editor dock

EpochEditorWidget: a Gantt-style view of the recording's epochs, one
draggable bar per epoch on a shared time axis.

- Drag a bar's left/right edge to move the epoch start/end.
- Click a bar's body to toggle whether the epoch is active.

Every edit mutates session.epochs in place and emits epochsChanged, so the
coordinator refreshes every dock that depends on the epoch table.  Wired
into MainWindow; this is the source of DataChange.EPOCHS.

With this, all 11 listed docks are live: preprocessing, HR, BP, Poincare,
epochs, PSD, profiles, spectrogram, transfer, transfer-profile and results.

Tested: bars render, edge-drag resizes, body-click toggles.  400 passing.
